## Special weapon mechanics

- Cannonballs bounce for chained damage
- Spraygun clouds hit multiple targets
- Twin-Cannon fires dual projectiles
- Automatic "Booster" = 3-way spread
- Power socket: 4200 capacity, 420/bolt (~9 charges)

## Skill system

- 1-8 skill points per tier per category
- Category unlocks at 9 skill points
- Deactivation delay: 3-85 game-time units by tier
